Privacy policy

This app is used only by its owner, for one purpose: uploading and managing Home Assistant backup files in the owner's own Google Drive.

  • It requests only the drive.file permission, so it can see only the files it creates, not anything else in your Drive.
  • No data is collected, sold, shared, or sent anywhere other than the owner's Google Drive.
  • No analytics, cookies, or tracking are used on this site.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
